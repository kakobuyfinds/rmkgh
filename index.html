How to use the LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

Confirm the exact variant

Save the original seller URL, color and size from the product listing. Compare the seller’s measurements with a garment or pair of shoes you already own. Record whether the chart uses body, garment, foot or insole measurements.

Check the warehouse photos

If LoveGoBuy accepts the seller link and provides quality-control photos, compare the item and size label with your order. Ask for a measurement photo if fit is uncertain, and check exchange terms before approving shipment.

Compare parcel quotes

Use the same destination, packed dimensions and weight for every quote. Check how each route calculates billable weight and whether the quote includes insurance, handling fees or destination charges.

Landed-cost worksheet

Enter your quotes in USD. Add any estimated duties or taxes under Fees and taxes.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do these products come from?

The featured products come from CAFANS. This independent guide links to CAFANS product pages. Verify agent compatibility and seller availability for each item before paying.

Do I need Excel to use the spreadsheet?

You can browse the table in your browser. Use the CSV download if you want to save the filtered rows for Excel or Google Sheets.

What if a product link stops working?

Open the matching CAFANS category and search for the model or product type. Compare the replacement listing’s exact size, color and seller terms before adding it to your shortlist.
